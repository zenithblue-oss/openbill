OpenBill is free, open-source GST billing and invoicing for Indian small businesses. Everything stays on your device: no account, no internet permission, no ads, no trackers.

Features
• GST invoices with live totals: CGST/SGST or IGST by place of supply
• Percentage or amount discounts, round-off, and additional charges such as freight
• Drafts, automatic numbering, and cancelling with a reason
• Parties and items with search and a recycle bin
• Offline GSTIN check-digit validation that fills in the state
• 12 original A4, A5 and thermal receipt templates with live previews
• Your logo, signature, accent colour, bank details, and a UPI QR code made on-device
• Built-in PDF viewer; share invoices as PDF
• Inventory: batches, serials, godowns, transfers, valuation and barcode labels
• Purchase bills, returns, quotations, proforma invoices and challans
• Payments with split tender, expenses, ageing and payment reminders
• Reports, GSTR-1 JSON, GSTR-3B, HSN summary, Tally XML export and books lock
• CSV import and export, voucher attachments, daily automatic backups, several businesses
• Material 3 design with light, dark and dynamic colour themes

Privacy
Camera is used only for barcode scanning and Bluetooth only for receipt printing. No internet permission. Your data never leaves the phone unless you share a PDF yourself.

Alpha software: please confirm GST treatment with your CA before relying on it for real books.
