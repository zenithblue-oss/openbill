OpenBill Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29. Developer: zenithblue. App: OpenBill (com.zenithblue.openbill), an offline GST billing and invoicing app.

Summary

OpenBill does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or business data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. OpenBill has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service. The Android app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

Data stored on your device

Your business details, parties, items, invoices, payments, and settings are stored in a local database on your device. We have no access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): used only to connect to a Bluetooth thermal printer for printing receipts.
  • Biometric / device lock: used only for the optional app lock. Biometric data is handled by your operating system and is never available to OpenBill.

Backups and exports

Backups are created only when you ask for them. They can be encrypted with a password you choose. You decide where a backup file is saved and whether to copy it elsewhere. Exports (PDF, CSV, GST files) and shared invoices are likewise created and sent only by your action, using apps you choose. We cannot recover a lost backup password.

Links

The "Privacy policy" entry in the app opens this page in your browser. Your browser, not OpenBill, makes that network request.

Children

OpenBill is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page with a new effective date and noted in the release notes at github.com/zenithblue-oss/openbill/releases. The full history is in the public repository.
