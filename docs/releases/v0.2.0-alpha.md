# OpenBill v0.2.0-alpha

Alpha: confirm GST treatment with your CA before relying on it for real books.

## Highlights
- **Inventory:** batches, serials, godowns, transfers, valuation, barcode labels and camera scanning; pick sale batches first-expiring first.
- **Documents:** purchase bills, returns, quotations, proforma, challans and conversion to invoice; duplicate, recycle bin, restore cancelled; additional charges.
- **Money:** payments (including split tender), expenses, contra, reconciliation, ageing, editable reminder templates.
- **Reports and GST:** report hub with CSV/JSON/PDF export, GSTR-1 JSON, GSTR-3B, HSN summary, Tally XML, books lock, financial-year close, audit log.
- **Data:** CSV import with dry-run, verified backup/restore, daily automatic backups (newest 7), multiple businesses, voucher attachments, offline HSN/SAC search.
- **Sharing/printing:** share invoice as PDF, PNG or text summary; party balance on documents.
- **Desktop and CLI:** Ctrl+K command palette, portable mode, bulk PDF export/print; `openbill` CLI for backup, db, invoices, payments, stock, reports and exports.

## Fixes
- Purchase documents label the party picker "Supplier".
- Split tender: choosing a bank-type mode selects a bank account.
- Opening stock rejected on batch/serial items (it duplicated the item on retry).
